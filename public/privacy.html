Privacy

Updated 20 September 2026

Resourcify has no accounts and no ads. It does not track you. It keeps no list of who used it. It does not save photos, location, answers, lists or messages. Not on our server, and not in your browser.

One thing to know: some features send what you enter to outside companies, like Google. This page says exactly when that happens. It only happens after you choose to use that feature and accept a notice.

Tools that stay on your phone

The supply list, the budget, the recycling math and the benefits finder all work inside your browser. Nothing is uploaded. Your answers stay in this tab so you can switch screens. To wipe them, tap Clear this session or reload the page.

Links to agencies open other websites. Those sites have their own privacy rules.

Photos

When you choose a photo and tap Analyze or Count containers, a notice asks if you accept. If you accept, the photo goes through our server to Google Gemini. Our server holds it only in memory while Google looks at it. We do not save the photo or the result to a file, a database or a log. The preview is removed after the analysis finishes.

We cannot promise the photo is wiped from memory the same second. It stays until the computer reuses that memory.

Google handles the photo under its own rules. We use a paid Google account for the pilot. Under Google’s paid terms, your photo is not used to train its products. Google may still keep it for a short time to check for abuse or to follow the law. We cannot delete anything Google holds. Read the Gemini API terms and Google’s usage policies.

Take photos of supplies only. Do not upload faces, ID cards, medical papers, or anything with a name on it. You can always type your list instead.

Location and the map

We only ask for your location when you tap Use my location. You can type coordinates instead. When you search, a notice asks if you accept. If you accept, your location and search words go through our server to Google Places. They travel inside the request, not in a web address.

Opening the Community Map or Cash for Cans loads the map, which connects your browser straight to Google Maps. Tapping a directions link sends the destination to Google. Google may then ask for your starting point. Asking for a walking estimate sends your starting point and the destination through our server to Google Routes.

If you tap Cancel on a notice, nothing is sent. If you choose Accept and don’t ask again, that lasts until you clear the session or reload. Anything already sent to Google cannot be called back.

When you add your location on the Rationer, it goes to Open-Meteo for today's weather and to Google Places to find nearby stores and food pantries. A notice asks first. We keep neither your location nor the results. Your budget, household size and typed items stay on your device; the budget plan is worked out there. See the Google Privacy Policy and Open-Meteo’s terms.

Asking the guide

The guide answers from a fixed set of text about San Diego County services. When you ask a question, it goes through our server to Google Gemini along with that text. The answer comes back to your screen. We do not save the question or the answer. The conversation disappears when you close the tab. Google handles the question under the terms linked above.

Type only what the question needs. Do not include your name, address, date of birth, case numbers or details about other people.

Before a question is sent, our server removes anything that looks like a Social Security number, a phone number, an email address, a date of birth or a long ID number. This is a safety net, not a promise. Names and everything else you type are sent as they are.

If a question mentions suicide, overdose, violence or a child being hurt, our server answers with hotline numbers right away. Those questions are not sent to Google. This works even when the guide is turned off. It is an automatic message, not a person. It is not a substitute for calling 988, 911 or 211.

Our hosting company

Resourcify runs on Render. Like any website host, Render sees the technical details needed to deliver pages, such as your IP address. Render may keep its own system logs. Our app does not log who made each request. Our rate limit counts total requests, not your requests. See Render’s privacy policy and Render’s logging guide. We cannot promise that a hosting provider collects no technical data.

Offline use

The app can save its own code, styles and icons on your phone so it opens without a signal. It does not save your uploads, searches or answers. It uses no tracking cookies. Your browser may remember permissions and history on its own.

When the app starts, it tries to remove leftover storage from older versions of Resourcify. If it cannot, it shows a notice asking you to close other tabs and clear site data. It does not remove records from an older deployment that used Firebase. Contact us about those.

Community reports and radio messages

Public community reporting and safe-location submissions are disabled in this pilot. There is no Firebase connection.

Radio messages go to your connected Meshtastic radio, not to our server. The last 100 show in this tab. Other radios and gateways may store or pass on your messages. We cannot promise they stay private or get deleted. Do not send personal details over the radio.

Who this pilot is for

This pilot is for adults 18 and over. Google’s rules for Gemini limit who can use it and where. Each organization running the pilot must check those rules before turning on photo analysis.
